# VibeMeeting 标识

项目 Logo 由内置 imagegen 生成：两个对话角色相互连接，形成 V 形轮廓，以蓝色和青绿色呼应会议界面。透明底图用于 README、账号页面和浏览器图标，maskable 图标使用白底。

- 原始设计：[`../images/vibemeeting-logo.png`](../images/vibemeeting-logo.png)
- 网页标识：`app/static/brand/vibemeeting-logo.png`
- 浏览器及 PWA 图标：`flutter_app/web/favicon.png`、`flutter_app/web/icons/`

最终生成提示词（内置工具模式）：

> Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: original logo icon for VibeMeeting, an open-source self-hosted browser meeting app. Create one polished, distinctive geometric symbol on a truly transparent background. Form a welcoming conversation bubble and a subtle V-shaped meeting connection from two interlocking rounded shapes; clear negative space, balanced silhouette, flat vector-like geometry, royal blue matching a modern meeting interface with a restrained teal accent. Center a single mark with comfortable padding on a square canvas. It must remain recognizable as a favicon at small sizes and look professional in an internal enterprise tool. No letters, no words, no watermark, no mockup, no gradients, no shadows, no border, no third-party or existing brand marks. Deliver one finished icon, not a sheet of concepts.
